Two of those terms are off the table and it’s worth saying why out loud. Washable is 32% of board share and the copy has never claimed it — MioTetto says “wipes clean,” never machine-washable. Rule 01

Spend the 75 characters on the category’s language, not the product’s construction

Modularity is a real differentiator with no established search vocabulary in this niche. It cannot earn a place in an entity label. It belongs in Item Highlights, bullet 1, and image slot 3.

Rule 02

Never write copy that a 4.40 rating disputes

Rank 49 of 60 on rating while sitting at $34.57 against a $37.11 median — priced below the middle of its market. There is no premium here to defend, and no rewrite that fixes satisfaction.

Rule 03

The review mass is the asset — every change happens on this ASIN

5,847 ratings on a 2020 launch, one of only seven pre-2022 products on a board the 2024 cohort now leads. That mass doesn’t transfer. No new ASIN, no family split, no re-key.

02Diagnosis

Retrieved, but under-ranked — with retrieval capacity thrown away

Absence doesn’t apply. The board proves presence at rank 15 of 60, so any recommendation that treats this as a discovery problem should be challenged on that basis.

What’s actually happening is that the listing has surrendered its two cheapest retrieval surfaces — a 477-byte backend field that is dead, and 125 characters of Item Highlights locked behind a 200-character title — while carrying enough internal contradiction that a system trying to reconcile the product with itself cannot. Two age windows. Two tile sizes. Waterproof against water-resistant. Three set counts. Underneath that sits a ranking constraint the copy cannot touch: 4.40 stars, 49th of 60.

03The gate

Stage zero never cleared, and that limits what can be tested

There is no Business Report in the pack, so out-of-stock, Buy Box share, Prime state and suppression have not been ruled out. The board reports an average of two sellers and vendors on this ASIN over 360 days, which is consistent with a second offer competing — but that is not a Buy Box figure and it is not being treated as one.

The consequence, stated plainly: a retrieval finding cannot be separated from an offer problem until that report exists, and no measured before/after test should be scheduled on this ASIN until Buy Box is confirmed above roughly 95%. Reports → Business Reports → Detail Page Sales and Traffic by Child Item closes this in five minutes.

04Findings, biggest first

Eight findings, ordered by what they cost

01

The backend search-terms field is de-indexed — catalogue-wide

477 bytes on one SKU row, 475 on another, against a 249-byte cap. Over the cap Amazon drops the whole field rather than the overflow. Thirty-two well-chosen terms, none of them indexed. Across the catalogue: 31 of 40 ASINs, median 479 bytes, worst 500.

Critical
02

200-character title locks Item Highlights — zero of 19 tile ASINs have it

Amazon’s cap is 75 including spaces, and the title must comply before Item Highlights can be added. There is no parallel path. So this listing is exposed to an unreviewed AI rewrite that Amazon says is being applied gradually, and shut out of 125 characters of confirmed-searchable field that compliant competitors already use. One open question: four catalogue ASINs show a populated Item Highlight alongside a 186-character title, which the gating rule says cannot happen. The flat file shows submitted values, not live ones — that needs five minutes on the live page to settle.

Critical
03

The listing contradicts itself on who the product is for

The title ends “Playmat for Babies 6-12 Months.” Bullet 5 opens on “play mat for babies 0–6 months.” Two mutually exclusive age windows in the same five bullets. Age Range Description is empty here while 12 of 18 peers fill it — and the peers disagree with each other: the identical 16-tile mat in another color reads 3+ Years with a 36-month minimum age. On a crawling mat, that removes it from the age filter the entire tummy-time half of this niche browses through.

Critical
04

The physical dimensions do not agree with each other, anywhere

The same 16-tile mat is described four ways: attributes say 48 × 48 inches, which needs 12-inch tiles; bullet 2 says 12.4-inch tiles, which makes 49.6; a sibling ASIN’s attributes say 50.4; and the two descriptions on this one ASIN say “12.4 x 12.4-inch” and “12 x 12-inch” respectively. Then the package: 12.01 inches wide, which a 12.4-inch tile does not fit into, and 5.0 inches tall against 6.4 inches of foam before 27 borders go in. Package weights for the identical mat run 1.2, 2.8 and 3.3 lb. On the 36-tile ASIN, item weight 3.3 Kilograms sits against package weight 5.0 Pounds — the item is heavier than its own box.

Critical
05

“Waterproof” in the copy, “Water Resistant” in the attributes

Bullet 3’s headline says WATERPROOF PLAY MAT. The Special Features attribute says Water Resistant. Amazon’s structured data disagrees with your bullets, and one of them is wrong. That belongs to whoever owns the product spec, not a copywriter. On a sibling SKU the same value is populated twice in one row, which suggests these were pasted rather than selected.

High
06

Bullet copy fails five of twelve rules before anyone reads it

All five bullets open with an emoji rather than the claim — the retrieval encoder reads an ordered bag of words and the first token is decoration. “Perfect” appears three times; advertisement register measured −1.82 on rank, second-worst of fifteen tested styles. Two more the checker can’t see: bullet 3 ends Use as tapete para bebes — Spanish in a US listing — and the description carries a UTF-8 apostrophe leak, your kidâs nursery, verified on seven ASINs.

High
07

Bullet blocks are shared verbatim across seven ASINs

Efficient right up until one is wrong, at which point it’s wrong in seven places — which is exactly what happened with the 12.4-inch tile size and the age contradiction. The claim sweep found 22 ASINs whose bullets assert an age window their own title does not.

High
08

Browse node — flagged, and deliberately not recommended

MioTetto sits in Puzzles / Puzzle Play Mats. On the board, 56 of 60 products carrying 94.4% of clicks sit in Activity & Entertainment / Baby Gyms & Playmats. The obvious read is a misfiled listing. Don’t act on it yet: the ASIN is on the board at 1.98%, so the node is demonstrably not filtering it out of search, and Average BSR: 1 means this is the number-one bestseller in its node. Moving trades a #1 badge in a small pond for roughly rank 15 in a large one. That is a real trade, not a hygiene fix — it goes last, tested on a sibling.

Hold
Checked, and not a problem

Worth stating so the findings above carry weight. The image stack is full — eight images plus a swatch, no empty slots. Attribute fill is 79 fields against a line median of 74, so this is not an under-filled listing; it is an inaccurately-filled one, which is a different and more expensive diagnosis. Title and Item Name match on the live SKU, so no submitted-but-unapplied fix is hiding here.

07Sequence

Ordered by lever tier, not by ease

One change per ASIN per window, because batched changes are unattributable. Hold ad spend constant, use the price band as control, read at 60–90 days, and write the decision rule down before it ships rather than after you’ve seen the data.

Window 1 · has a clock on it

Title and Item Highlights, together

The 200-character title is exposed to an unreviewed AI rewrite right now, and every day it stays over 75 characters is a day Item Highlights is locked. This is the only item on the list with a deadline set by someone other than you.

Window 1 · same pass

The backend field, across all 31 ASINs at once

477 bytes to 246. This isn’t really a second change — it repairs a field currently contributing nothing, so there’s no before-state to contaminate. Catalogue hygiene, not an experiment. The rebuild also strips four competitor brand names, four Spanish terms and seven “wooden” variants sitting on two mesh playards.

Window 2

The attributes

Age Range Description filled and made consistent; the 36-month minimum corrected on two color variants; tile size and package dimensions settled to one true value; Item Thickness populated with the 0.4 inches the copy has claimed for years without an attribute behind it; weight units normalized. Free, no downside, and the highest-weighted controllable lever there is.

Window 3

The shared bullet block

Fix once, push to all seven ASINs. Removes the age contradiction, the Spanish, the emoji front-load and the set-count conflict in a single action.

Window 4

Images — slots 2, 3 and 6

Slot 2 becomes a spec panel carrying the numbers a buyer filters on, because those are precisely the numbers the flat file disagrees with itself about. Slot 3 carries modularity, the differentiator that can’t buy title characters and has to be won on the page. Slot 6 carries the non-toxic proof — the copy says “certified materials” and no certification attribute exists anywhere in the flat file.

Later — and only on a sibling

The browse node

Never on this ASIN. See finding 08.

08Limits

What this audit could not do, and what closes each gap

Missing · changes the ranking

Search Terms tab

Only the Products tab was supplied. Terms should be ranked by modeled units — volume × conversion — never volume, because the highest-volume term in a niche is frequently among the worst-converting. The term table above ranks by share of board clicks carried by titles containing the term, which tells you what the winners say, not what converts. Weaker, and labeled as such.

Missing · changes the verdict

Search Query Performance

No own-name share, so no rewrite-pays verdict. Under 30% means the listing is the lever and effort compounds; over 50% means a rewrite defends share it already has and cannot grow the category. Which one this is changes how you’d read any test result here.

Missing · probably holds the answer

The live page

Two findings unavailable: the ranked review lexicon with sentiment, and the A+ alt-text layer. On a product rated 4.40 with 5,847 ratings, the review lexicon is very likely where the real answer sits. It is the single most valuable thing left to pull.

Missing · gates the test

Business Report
