What changed, beyond the byte count

Trimming to fit would have kept the real defects. Reading the fields turned up four problems that cost more than the overflow did.

Removed

Competitor brand names

The quilted mats were buying baby einstein, baby einstein toys and fisher price play mat. Baby Einstein is rank 3 on the board at 5.38% share. That traffic is not addressable by your listing. The playards carried harppa baby playpen.

Removed

Terms for a different product

All four playard ASINs — two mesh, two wooden — carried the identical 499-byte block, so the breathable-mesh playpens were indexed for seven wooden and wood variants. The same shared-block habit as the bullets.

Removed

Spanish, artifacts and contradictions

tapete para bebes, corrales para bebes and corral para bebe on US listings. baby registry search gift registry by name is a search query, not a keyword. Fields carried both 0-6 months and 6-12 months, and the 74×74 mat carried 50x50 playpen mat.

Why the rebuilt fields are single words

Amazon's lexical index matches token combinations, so foam floor tiles; foam mat; interlocking foam tiles; foam tiles spends the word foam four times and buys nothing extra. A deduplicated token set covers the same phrases in roughly a third of the bytes. Words already in the title are excluded too — they are indexed from the title, and repeating them is spent capacity. That is where most of the reclaimed bytes came from, not from dropping terms.

Deliberately not included — and two things needing your sign-off
  • washable carries 32% of board click share. The listings say “wipes clean”, never machine-washable. Not ours to claim.
  • thick carries 48% of board share. This mat is 0.4in against a 1.3in reference product in the niche — a fight lost on the detail page.
  • waterproof and reversible on the quilted mats: that group's copy never makes either claim.
  • Finished dimensions on the tile mats: the flat file gives 48×48, 50.4×50.4 and 12.4in tiles for the same product. Do not index a number nobody has verified.
  • Sign-off 1. tummy time carries 33% of board click share and the quilted-mat group never claims it. One line in the bullets would legitimize it in the backend. That is a new claim, so it is your call.
  • Sign-off 2. Confirm the mesh playards genuinely fold before anyone re-adds foldable — their copy does not currently say so.
