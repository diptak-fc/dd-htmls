Targeting strategy: piggyback on their visibility

product-targeting priority by how beatable each ASIN is

We can't out-rank the category leaders organically yet, so PPC has to do the work of borrowing their traffic. Split spend into two very different plays: conquest the weak/very-weak ASINs with product-targeting — they're beatable and some (Culiex, Greener Chef) are still pulling real volume despite weak listings — and intercept the strong ones with Sponsored Brand/Display placements on their PDPs, since we can win clicks there long before we can win rank.
